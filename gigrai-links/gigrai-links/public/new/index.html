Questions

Straight answers before you apply.

What is GigrAI?

A free search engine for remote AI jobs. We pull live roles from AI platforms like micro1 and Mercor into one place, so you can find work that fits your skills without checking every site.

Is this legit?

Yes. Every role comes straight from the platform that's hiring, and you apply on their own site. They're all free to join. If anyone ever asks you to pay to get a job, it's a scam, so walk away.

How up to date are the roles?

The search refreshes every 30 minutes. New roles appear by themselves, closed ones disappear, and each role shows when it was posted.

Do I apply through GigrAI?

No. Tapping Apply takes you to the platform's own job page. They handle the application, any assessment, your contract and your pay.

Do I need AI experience?

Usually not. Most roles want skills you already have, like writing, coding, research or knowing a subject well. Each listing says what's needed.

Can I apply from my country?

Choose where you live in the search. Roles that say they're open to your country show a green dot. Where a platform doesn't say, you'll see "Check eligibility", so check the listing before you apply.

How do I get paid?

You're paid by the platform you work for, not by GigrAI. Pay rates on each role come from the platform. Payment methods vary, so check their payment page before you start.

Why is GigrAI free?

Some links are referral links, so we may get a reward from the platform if you're hired. It costs you nothing and doesn't change your pay.

Can't find a role that fits?

The search only shows roles that are live right now, and new ones are added all the time. Join our WhatsApp channel to hear about them first.

Where are my saved roles kept?

Only on this phone or browser. There's no account and nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.
