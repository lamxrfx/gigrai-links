Privacy & cookies

Last updated 21 October 2026 (adverts added)

The short version

  • You don't need an account to search, and we don't ask for your name or email to use the site.
  • You don't need an account to search. We only ask for your email if you sign up for job alerts, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Saved roles stay on your own phone or browser.
  • Some apply links are referral links. They cost you nothing and don't change your pay.
  • We show Google adverts, which use cookies. If you're in the UK or EEA, you choose what you allow.

Who we are

GigrAI (remotegigrai.com) is a free search engine for remote AI jobs. We list roles from platforms such as micro1 and Mercor. We are not the employer and we don't handle applications, contracts or pay. When you apply, you do it on the platform's own site under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

  • Basic usage data: our host, Cloudflare, records anonymous technical data such as the pages requested, search terms, which links are tapped and your approximate country and town (worked out from your connection, not GPS). We use it to keep the site working and to see which roles are useful. We also keep anonymous daily totals (for example, page views per country, or clicks on a sponsored role) and may share those totals with advertisers. They never include anything that identifies you. To see how many people are on the site at once, we keep a short-lived record of recent activity by town, using a code that changes every day instead of your IP address. It's deleted after about 15 minutes.
  • Saved roles: stored in your browser's local storage on your device. We never receive them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Country filter: if you choose a country, that choice is kept on your device.

We don't sell personal data.

Email job alerts

If you sign up for job alerts, we collect:

  • your email address, and the optional choices you make: the country you live in, the kinds of work you're interested in and a minimum pay;
  • a record of your consent: when you signed up, which page you used, the wording you agreed to, your IP address at the time, and when you confirmed by email.

We use this only to send you job alerts and GigrAI news you asked for (our lawful basis is your consent), to match roles to you, and to see which emails are useful. Emails may include referral links and clearly labelled sponsored roles.

Your details are stored by our email provider, MailerLite (UAB "MailerLite", Lithuania, EU), which processes them on our behalf. We don't sell or share your email with advertisers.

You can unsubscribe with one click from any email. We delete your details from our list if you unsubscribe or don't confirm your sign-up, and remove inactive subscribers after 24 months without opening an email.

The GigrAI assistant

The chat assistant is an AI that answers questions about GigrAI and suggests live roles. When you send a message, the conversation so far and the country you chose (or the one your connection suggests) go to an AI provider to write the reply. That's Cloudflare Workers AI, or Anthropic (Claude) if we switch to it. We don't store your messages or link them to you. We only count how many replies are sent each day. Your conversation stays in this browser tab and clears when you close it.

Please don't type personal details like your email, phone number or ID into the chat. The assistant can make mistakes, so check anything important on the platform's own page.

Referral links

Some links to job platforms include our referral code. If you join or are hired through one, the platform may reward us. It costs you nothing, and your pay and treatment are exactly the same.

Adverts and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show adverts, which helps keep GigrAI free. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • If you're in the UK or EEA, you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we don't run accounts, we usually hold nothing that identifies you, apart from your email if you've signed up for alerts. To ask a question or make a request, email partners@remotegigrai.com or message us on Instagram at @gigr.ai. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
